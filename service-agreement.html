1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Nova Food Trailers ("Broker") provides the following services as an independent intermediary:

  1. Manufacturer Sourcing & Quality Verification: Broker connects Buyer with verified, third-party food trailer manufacturers ("Manufacturer"). Broker inspects and verifies manufacturer quality standards on behalf of Buyer.
  2. Configuration & Advisory: Broker assists Buyer in selecting trailer size, equipment, and specifications suited to Buyer's business needs.
  3. Logistics Coordination: Broker coordinates shipping and delivery of the trailer from Manufacturer to Buyer's specified delivery point, including procurement of transit insurance.
  4. Digital Services (if applicable): Broker may provide branding, wrapping design, logo creation, website development, and/or marketing services as separately agreed.
  5. Permit & Inspection Advisory: Broker assists with scheduling Health Department inspections and advises on permit requirements. Broker does not guarantee permit approval, as this is subject to local government authority.

2. ROLE OF BROKER — NOT A MANUFACTURER OR SELLER

Buyer acknowledges and agrees that:

  1. Nova Food Trailers acts solely as a broker and service intermediary, NOT as the manufacturer, builder, or direct seller of the food trailer.
  2. The food trailer is manufactured by an independent third-party Manufacturer. Broker facilitates the transaction but is not a party to the manufacturing contract.
  3. Broker exercises reasonable care in selecting and verifying Manufacturers, but does not guarantee the Manufacturer's work, materials, or compliance beyond the scope of Broker's verification process.
  4. Any warranty on the trailer is provided by the Manufacturer directly. Broker will assist Buyer in facilitating warranty claims but is not the warrantor.
