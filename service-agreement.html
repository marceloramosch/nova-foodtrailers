1. SCOPE OF SERVICES Both Parties

Nova Food Trailers ("Broker") provides the following services as an independent intermediary:

  1. Manufacturer Sourcing & Quality Verification: Broker connects Buyer with verified, third-party food trailer manufacturers ("Manufacturer"). Broker inspects and verifies manufacturer quality standards on behalf of Buyer.
  2. Configuration & Advisory: Broker assists Buyer in selecting trailer size, equipment, and specifications suited to Buyer's business needs.
  3. Logistics Coordination: Broker coordinates shipping and delivery of the trailer from Manufacturer to Buyer's specified delivery point within the continental United States, including procurement of transit insurance.
  4. Digital Services (if applicable): Broker may provide branding, wrapping design, logo creation, website development, and/or marketing services as separately agreed in writing.
  5. Permit & Inspection Advisory: Broker assists with scheduling Health Department inspections and advises on permit requirements. Broker does not guarantee permit approval, as this is subject to local government authority.

2. ROLE OF BROKER Protects Broker

Buyer acknowledges and agrees that:

  1. Nova Food Trailers acts solely as a broker and service intermediary, NOT as the manufacturer, builder, or direct seller of the food trailer.
  2. The food trailer is manufactured by an independent third-party Manufacturer. Broker facilitates the transaction but is not a party to the manufacturing contract.
  3. Broker exercises reasonable care in selecting and verifying Manufacturers, but does not guarantee the Manufacturer's work, materials, or compliance beyond the scope of Broker's verification process.
  4. Any warranty on the trailer is provided by the Manufacturer directly. Broker will actively assist Buyer in facilitating warranty claims but is not the warrantor.

3. BROKER'S OBLIGATIONS TO BUYER Protects Buyer

Broker commits to the following obligations:

  1. Manufacturer Transparency: Broker shall disclose to Buyer the identity and location of the Manufacturer producing their trailer upon request.
  2. Quality Oversight: Broker shall conduct quality checks during the build process and document the trailer's progress through photos and/or videos shared with Buyer on a weekly basis.
  3. Specification Compliance: Broker is responsible for ensuring that the Manufacturer receives the correct specifications as agreed with Buyer. If the delivered trailer materially deviates from the agreed specifications, Broker shall, at Buyer's election: (i) coordinate with Manufacturer to correct the deficiency at no additional cost to Buyer; or (ii) arrange a full refund of all payments made by Buyer.
  4. Delivery Guarantee: If Broker fails to deliver a trailer to Buyer within 16 weeks of deposit confirmation (absent force majeure events), Buyer may demand a full refund of all payments, which Broker shall remit within 14 business days.
  5. Fund Accountability: Broker shall provide Buyer with written receipts for all payments received and, upon request, evidence that funds have been applied toward the trailer order (e.g., Manufacturer payment confirmations).
  6. NSF & Health Department Standards: Broker shall ensure the trailer is built to meet NSF equipment standards and is configured to pass Health Department inspection in Buyer's county/state. If the trailer fails its initial Health Department inspection due to build specifications that were Broker's responsibility, Broker shall coordinate and cover the cost of corrections.
  7. VIN & Documentation: Broker shall provide Buyer with the trailer's VIN, serial number, title documentation, and manufacturer's certificate of origin (MCO) necessary for registration and financing.
